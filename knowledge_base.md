## 3. Services

### 3.1 Child Care

**Description:** Reliable, loving in-home care for newborns to 2-year-olds.

**Details:**
- In-home care (caregiver comes to your home)
- Up to 4 hours daily
- One-on-one attention for infants and toddlers
- Children stay in their familiar home environment — no transport needed

**Qualifications:**
- 30 hours training at Kingwood Christian Childhood Center
- CPR, AED, and First Aid certified
- Daycare department head experience

**Best for:** Parents with infants and toddlers needing part-time, in-home childcare

---

### 3.2 Errand Running

**Description:** Reliable and efficient errand running to save time and reduce stress.

**What's included:**
- Grocery shopping (with delivery or in-home stocking)
- Prescription pickups and delivery
- Mail and package handling
- Business-to-business errands

**Best for:** Busy individuals and businesses needing outsourced task management

---

### 3.3 Tax Preparation

**Description:** Professional tax preparation for individuals filing W-2 forms.

**What's included:**
- Accurate return preparation
- Timely submission
- Focus on maximizing refunds and minimizing liabilities

**Important:** W-2 income only — not for self-employed or complex returns

**Best for:** Working individuals with W-2 employment income
